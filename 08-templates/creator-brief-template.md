## Section 4 - The two voice registers and which one the creator uses

The brand speaks in two registers (full detail in `00-foundations/brand-voice.md`). Most creators use Register B (customer testimonial).

### If you are a customer / UGC creator / affiliate sharing your own experience

You are in Register B. Use the **5-beat honest-attribution formula** (`08-templates/testimonial-template.md`):

1. State the discipline + goal you were already doing
2. Disclose what the product did NOT cause (your outcome came from your work)
3. Name the specific failure mode the product prevented (where you would have quit)
4. Optional: a treat-within-the-frame
5. Optional: community identity close

The product caught the moment you would have quit. The product did not cause the result.

### If you are a podcast host introducing the founder

You are in Register A. Use the **founder statement cuts** (`08-templates/founder-statement.md`). Pick the cut that matches the slot length. Do not invent founder backstory.

## Section 5 - Hard rules (CI-enforced on copy you write for us)

- Short hyphens only. No em-dashes, no en-dashes.
- No emojis in any copy you draft for us.
- No exclamation marks.
- No medicinal vocabulary about the product ({{ BANNED_MEDICINAL_LIST }}).
- No category comparisons ("better than {{ COMPETITOR }}" / "vs {{ COMPETITOR }}").
- No founder labelled by specific lifestyle / diet / identity. The founder did the hard thing for {{ TIMEFRAME }}. That is the credential. Specific lifestyle stays in long-form interview content only.
- Co-founder names per the on-camera policy in `00-foundations/founder-stories.md`. Some co-founders are not on camera; do not include them in script even by reference.
- Every external claim traces to {{ EVIDENCE_LIBRARY_LOCATION }} or a primary source. Numbers without source are banned.

## Section 6 - What to send back

For review before posting:

- Script in full (or storyboard if visual-heavy)
- Captions / on-screen text
- Hook line (first 3 seconds for short-form video)
- CTA line

We reply within {{ REVIEW_SLA_HOURS }} hours. We flag voice violations; we do not rewrite your voice. If a line crosses Wall 1 or Wall 2, we send it back with the specific rule and let you fix it.

## Section 7 - Three worked testimonial examples

Read these before drafting. They are the model.

### Example A - Long-form cutting / weight goal

> Beat 1: "I have been cutting for 14 months to drop from 85 kg to my target weight before a deadline I set myself."
>
> Beat 2: "The 18 kg came off because I count macros and I do not skip the gym. {{ BRAND_NAME }} did not cause that."
>
> Beat 3: "What would have stopped me was the same meal every day. By week 3 the food tastes like nothing and the whole thing falls apart. {{ BRAND_NAME }} is what made the necessary meal taste like a meal."
>
> Beat 5: "I am {{ COMMUNITY_NAME_SINGULAR }} now."

### Example B - Endurance / event goal

> Beat 1: "I have been training for a marathon on a plant-based diet I picked for my own reasons."
>
> Beat 2: "The training plan got me there. {{ BRAND_NAME }} did not run the kilometres for me."
>
> Beat 3: "What I would have failed on was the food. Plant-based marathon training food is bland by default. {{ BRAND_NAME }} is what kept the lunches and dinners actually eatable through 14 weeks of mileage."
>
> Beat 4: "I added it to oats and to roast vegetables. Same little stick. Different meal."

### Example C - Travel / context goal

> Beat 1: "I travel four days a week and I have been holding my routine for 8 months."
>
> Beat 2: "The discipline came from me. {{ BRAND_NAME }} did not protect me from a single bad day."
>
> Beat 3: "What it did protect me from was the airport hotel meal that always broke the streak. I carry {{ BRAND_NAME }} now. The hotel breakfast tastes less like a punishment. I do not abandon the routine."
