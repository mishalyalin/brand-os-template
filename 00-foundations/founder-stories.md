## Two construction rules before you write any founder story

### Rule 1: chronological correctness (founder transforms WITHOUT the product)

The founder almost always succeeded at the thing the brand now helps with - BEFORE the brand existed. They did it the hard way, no shortcuts, no product to lean on. The product comes from watching others try and fail at the same thing without the founder's specific stubbornness or context.

Pattern:

> "{{ FOUNDER_1_NAME }} did {{ THE_HARD_THING }} the hard way for years. {{ FOUNDER_1_NAME }} kept trying to help people on theirs, and most quit by week {{ N }}. Not because of willpower. Because {{ THE_SPECIFIC_FAILURE_MODE }}. {{ FOUNDER_1_NAME }} built the tool they did not have, for everyone they tried to bring along."

This frame gives the founder Authority (they lived the discipline), Empathy (they watched others quit), and an Inventor's voice (they built the thing they wished they had). The brand is the post-facto rescue tool for the next person - not the founder's own crutch.

**Failure pattern to avoid:** stories that imply the founder used the product to achieve the founder's own outcome. That makes the product the cause of the outcome, which is a Wall-1 claim (medicinal / outcome-promising). It is also factually wrong almost every time.

### Rule 2: ICP-defensiveness on founder labels

Do not label the founder by specific lifestyle / diet / identity in customer copy if that label polarizes a meaningful share of the ICP. Use customer-owned framing instead.

| Polarizing | Customer-owned (use this) |
|---|---|
| "Vegan founder" | "Founder on their own diet" |
| "Keto founder" | "Founder with their own discipline" |
| "Marathoner founder" | "Founder with their own goal" |
| "Christian / Muslim / [religion] founder" | "Founder with their own private reason" |

The founder's specific lifestyle stays in long-form interview content where the reader has self-selected to hear the detail. It does not appear in hero / manifesto / paid-ad creative where it can flip a stranger against the brand before they hear the rest.

This is a structural defence, not modesty - the brand is for anyone doing the discipline, not for people who already share the founder's specific path.

## Co-founder / on-camera policy

Some co-founders are operational on camera. Others are by-equity-only with their own businesses or private lives. The on-camera policy is binary and should be captured during onboarding.

| Co-founder | On-camera in customer copy? | Notes |
|---|---|---|

| {{ COFOUNDER_2_NAME }} | {{ COFOUNDER_2_ON_CAMERA }} | {{ COFOUNDER_2_NOTES }} |

By-equity-only co-founders may be acknowledged in copy or credits but should never appear in hero, manifesto, ad creative, podcast circuit, or named scripts. Treat the on-camera policy as a hard rule; it does not flex for any single piece of content.
